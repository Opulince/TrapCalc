About this simulator

This is a combat simulator for Lords Mobile trap accounts. It models a solo march or a full rally hitting your garrison, and reports which troops die, which survive, what your wall and traps absorbed, and where your casualties land between the infirmary and the sanctuary.

How it's calibrated

The engine is fitted against real battle reports, not guesswork. One report established that casualties climb the tier ladder globally — every T4 squad took zero losses while T1 was wiped and T2 bled 23% — and the engine was rewritten to match. It currently reproduces that report's total losses to within 2% and its tier distribution to within half a percentage point.

What is still guesswork

Base per-tier troop stats, the phalanx and wedge damage split, the wall HP conversion and the trap base stats are estimates. Army morale is a modelling device, not a real game mechanic. Treat relative comparisons as reliable and absolute numbers as indicative.
