About this simulator

This is a combat simulator for Lords Mobile trap accounts. It models a solo march or a full rally hitting your garrison, and reports which troops die, which survive, what your wall and traps absorbed, and where your casualties land between the infirmary and the sanctuary.

How it's calibrated

The engine is fitted against six real battle reports, not guesswork. They showed that every troop type fights as four squads, each holding a quarter of every tier: a defending ranged wedge lost exactly half its T1 and T2 ranged, and an attacker lost exactly two whole infantry squads. Losses land on the formation's front squads first, lowest tier first. Four of the six reports are reproduced squad by squad to within about 5%, a fifth to within 13%; the sixth, a long fight against a max account, is the known weak spot.

What is still guesswork

Base per-tier troop stats, trap damage and the wall HP conversion are estimates — wall-up fights with traps are the least reliable part. Morale is a real mechanic but its exact drain is modelled, not known. Attacker stats never appear in a report, so predictions depend on your guess of the hitter's stats. Treat relative comparisons as reliable and absolute numbers as indicative.
