Engine Reference

Counter triangle: Infantry > Ranged > Cavalry > Infantry. A counter doubles damage dealt against the countered type. A phalanx puts one squad in front and eats 72% of the incoming hit; a wedge fields two (the anchor plus the type it counters, which is exactly what hard-counters the anchor's counter), splitting 55/45 and letting both fight at full output. An attacker wedge spreads its hit wider (55% front share) and gives two spearhead types +15% damage. Inside every squad T2 absorbs, then T4, then T5. Morale drains from lead-squad attrition and layer collapses, throttling defender output down to 35%. Familiar talents fire every 3rd round. Defender wins by wiping the march — or by killing enough of it to trip the retreat threshold before morale hits zero.
