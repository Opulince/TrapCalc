Privacy

This site does not ask you to sign up, and it does not store anything you type into the simulator. Every calculation runs entirely in your browser; your troop counts and stats are never sent anywhere.

Your inputs are kept in the page address after the #, so a reload keeps them and "Copy link" can share them. Browsers never send that part of the address to a server. If you share a link, whoever opens it sees the troop counts and stats in it.

Analytics

This site uses no cookies and does not fingerprint or track visitors across sites. There is no Google Analytics and no advertising cookies. If aggregate page-view analytics is enabled, it is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not track individual visitors.

Changes

If advertising is ever added, this page will be updated before it goes live, and any consent requirements will be honoured at that time.
