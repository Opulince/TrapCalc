Engine Reference

Counter triangle: Infantry > Ranged > Cavalry > Infantry; a counter doubles damage against the countered type. Each army fights in lines: the lineup's front type (a wedge adds the type it counters), then the other combat types, then siege at the very back. A line is only hit once the one in front of it is gone, and a finishing blow does not carry over — so a few thousand siege can keep a march from being wiped and its leader captured. Inside a line, damage spreads over every troop and lower tiers die fastest. Front squads fight at full output, the rest at 70%. While the wall stands, traps strike first; spikes, towers and logs each hit their counter type twice as hard, and siege hits traps twice as hard. Both armies have morale; at 0% an army retreats. Up to 15 rounds; familiar talents fire every 3rd round. Fitted to two real battle reports — see the calibration notes for what is confirmed and what is still estimated.
